> Resumen de Apple: Live Photos lets people capture favorite memories in a sound- and motion-rich interactive experience that adds vitality to traditional still photos.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- When Live Photos is available, the Camera app captures additional content — including audio and extra frames — before and after people take a photo.

### Best practices

- **Apply adjustments to all frames.**
- **Keep Live Photo content intact.**
- **Implement a great photo sharing experience.**
- **Clearly indicate when a Live Photo is downloading and when the photo is playable.**
- **Display Live Photos as traditional photos in environments that don’t support Live Photos.**
- **Make Live Photos easily distinguishable from still photos.**
- In cases where movement isn’t possible, show a system-provided badge above the photo, either with or without text.
- **Keep badge placement consistent.**

### Platform considerations

- *No additional considerations for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or tvOS.

#### visionOS

- In visionOS, people can view a Live Photo, but they can’t capture one.
