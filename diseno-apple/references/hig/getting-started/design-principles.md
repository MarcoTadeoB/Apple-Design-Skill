- The most successful and enduring designs are based on a deep understanding of how people think, feel, and interact with the world.

#### Purpose

- **Make something meaningful.**
- Design starts with intention.

#### Agency

- **Let people do things their own way.**
- An interface exists to help people accomplish their goals.

#### Responsibility

- **Act in people’s best interest.**
- Your work has an impact on people’s lives.

#### Familiarity

- **Build on what people know.**
- Drawing on concepts people already understand helps them feel immediately at home.

#### Flexibility

- **Adapt to diverse contexts and needs.**
- People use your software in ways as unique as they are.

#### Simplicity

- **Be clear and direct.**
- A well-designed experience removes the unnecessary, with every element earning its place.

#### Craft

- **Care about every detail.**
- Your design is a reflection of how much you care.

#### Delight

- **Make it human.**
- People remember how a product makes them feel.

### Purpose

- **Create value.**
- **Keep focused.**
- **Find new ways to solve the problem.**

### Agency

- **Stay out of the way.**
- **Give people the freedom to explore.**
- **Help people recover from mistakes.**

### Responsibility

- **Be fully transparent about what your product does and why.**
- **Keep people’s information safe.**

### Familiarity

- **Use concepts that people know.**
- **Keep visuals and interactions consistent**
- **Provide clear feedback.**

### Flexibility

- **Design for everyone.**
- **Preserve a person’s context.**
- **Consider a variety of input methods.**
- **Approach every platform with intention.**

### Simplicity

- **Include just what’s necessary.**
- **Be concise.**
- **Establish hierarchy.**

### Craft

- **Quality sets the tone.**
- **Experiment and iterate.**
- **Maintain your craft.**

### Delight

- **Identify the emotion you want to inspire.**
- **Create defining moments.**
- **Don’t mistake delight for decoration.**
- **Consider the whole.**
