> Resumen de Apple: A status bar appears along the upper edge of the screen and displays information about the device’s current state, like the time, cellular carrier, and battery level.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
### Best practices

- **Obscure content under the status bar.**
- **Consider temporarily hiding the status bar when displaying full-screen media.**
- **Avoid permanently hiding the status bar.**

### Platform considerations

- *No additional considerations for iOS or iPadOS.
