# Dock menus — HIG (condensado)

- URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dock-menus
- Descargado: 2026-10-15
- Sección: components › Menus and actions
- Plataformas: macos
- Última actualización: sin registro de cambios

> Resumen de Apple: On a Mac, people can secondary click an app’s or game’s icon in the Dock to reveal a Dock menu, which presents both system-provided and custom items.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- The system-provided Dock menu items can vary depending on whether the app is open.
> **Note:** Although iOS and iPadOS don’t support a Dock menu, people can reveal a similar menu of system-provided and custom items — called Home Screen quick actions — when they long press an app icon on the Home Screen or in the Dock.

### Best practices

- As with all menus, you need to label Dock menu items succinctly and organize them logically.
- **Make custom Dock menu items available in other places, too.**
- **Prefer high-value custom items for your Dock menu.**

### Platform considerations

- *Not supported in iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS.*
