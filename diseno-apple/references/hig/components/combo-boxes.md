> Resumen de Apple: A combo box combines a text field with a pull-down button in a single control.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- People can enter a custom value into the field or click the button to choose from a list of predefined values.

### Best practices

- **Populate the field with a meaningful default value from the list.**
- **Use an introductory label to let people know what types of items to expect.**
- **Provide relevant choices.**
- **Make sure list items aren’t wider than the text field.**
