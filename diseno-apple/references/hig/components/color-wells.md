> Resumen de Apple: A color well lets people adjust the color of text, shapes, guides, and other onscreen elements.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- A color well displays a color picker when people tap or click it.

### Best practices

- **Consider the system-provided color picker for a familiar experience.**

### Platform considerations

- *No additional considerations for iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS.

#### macOS

- When people click a color well, it receives a highlight to provide visual confirmation that it’s active.
- Color wells also support drag and drop, so people can drag colors from one color well to another, and from the color picker to a color well.
