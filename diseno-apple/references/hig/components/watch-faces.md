> Resumen de Apple: A watch face is a view that people choose as their primary view in watchOS.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- The watch face is at the heart of the watchOS experience.
- In watchOS 7 and later, people can share the watch faces they configure.
- You can also configure a watch face to share from within your app, on your website, or through Messages, Mail, or social media.

### Best practices

- **Help people discover your app by sharing watch faces that feature your complications.**
- **Display a preview of each watch face you share.**
- **Aim to offer shareable watch faces for all Apple Watch devices.**
- **Respond gracefully if people choose an incompatible watch face.**

### Platform considerations

- *Not supported in iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, or visionOS.*
