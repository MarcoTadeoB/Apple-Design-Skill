# Rating indicators — HIG (condensado)

- URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/rating-indicators
- Descargado: 2026-10-15
- Sección: components › Status
- Plataformas: macos
- Última actualización: 2022-09-23 — New page.

> Resumen de Apple: A rating indicator uses a series of horizontally arranged graphical symbols — by default, stars — to communicate a ranking level.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- A rating indicator doesn’t display partial symbols; it rounds the value to display complete symbols only.

### Best practices

- **Make it easy to change rankings.**
- **If you replace the star with a custom symbol, make sure that its purpose is clear.**

### Platform considerations

- *No additional considerations for macOS.
