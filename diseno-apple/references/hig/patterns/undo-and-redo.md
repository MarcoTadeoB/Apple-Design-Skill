# Undo and redo — HIG (condensado)

- URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/undo-and-redo

- Sección: patterns
- Plataformas: ios,ipados,macos,visionos
- Última actualización: sin registro de cambios

> Resumen de Apple: Undo and redo gives people easy ways to reverse many types of actions, which can also help people explore and experiment safely as they learn a new interface or task.

_Formato: las frases en **negrita** son las reglas literales de Apple (citas cortas);
«…» son frases literales con valores numéricos; las tablas son las especificaciones
originales. Para el texto completo, abre la URL._
- People expect undo and redo to let them reverse their recent actions, so they’re likely to try undoing — often multiple times — until something changes.

### Best practices

- **Help people predict the results of undo and redo as much as possible.**
- **Show the results of an undo or redo.**
- **Let people undo multiple times.**
- **Consider giving people the option to revert multiple changes at once.**
- **Provide undo and redo buttons only when necessary.**

### Platform considerations

- *No additional considerations for visionOS.

#### iOS, iPadOS

- **Avoid redefining standard gestures for undo and redo.**
- **Briefly and precisely describe the operation to be undone or redone.**

#### macOS

- **Place undo and redo commands in the Edit menu and support the standard keyboard shortcuts.**
