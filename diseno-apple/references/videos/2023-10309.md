> Descripción de Apple: Bring your widgets to watchOS with the new Smart Stack. We'll show you how to use standard design layouts, color and iconography, and signal-based relevancy to ensure your app's widgets are glanceable, distinctive and smart. When you're ready to make your own, watch this code-along: "Build widgets for the Smart Stack on watchOS"
