> Descripción de Apple: Learn how to identify, load, display, and create High Dynamic Range (HDR) still images in your app. Explore common HDR concepts and find out about the latest updates to the ISO specification. Learn how to identify and display HDR images with SwiftUI and UIKit, create them from ProRAW and RAW captures, and display them in CALayers. We'll also take you through CoreGraphics support for ISO HDR and share best practices for HDR adoption.
