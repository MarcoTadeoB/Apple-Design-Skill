> Descripción de Apple: Discover the latest improvements and new APIs in Apple Pay. You'll discover enhancements to the Apple Pay eCommerce experience, like the new dynamic payment button, and learn how to take advantage of enhanced support for preauthorized payments. We'll cover brand-new features coming to order tracking in Wallet, and recommendations to help your orders look their best. We'll also dive into FinanceKit's new background delivery APIs, allowing financial management apps to get the latest data, even when they're not active.
