> Descripción de Apple: SwiftUI now offers powerful tools to level up your layouts and arrange views for your app's interface. We'll introduce you to the Grid container, which helps you create highly customizable, two-dimensional layouts, and show you how you can use the Layout protocol to build your own containers with completely custom behavior. We'll also explore how you can create seamless animated transitions between your layout types, and share tips and best practices for creating great interfaces.
