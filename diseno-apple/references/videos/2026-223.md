> Descripción de Apple: Elevate your app experience with Live Activities. Explore many of the places where Live Activities appear, including a new style in the Dynamic Island that delivers more information when iPhone is used in landscape. Learn how to tailor your Live Activity for each space, structure your content and data, and drive real time updates from start to finish using ActivityKit and push notifications.
