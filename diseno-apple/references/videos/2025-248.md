> Descripción de Apple: Design generative AI experiences that leverage the strengths of the Foundation Models framework. We'll start by showing how to design prompts for the on-device large language model at the core of Apple Intelligence. Then, we'll introduce key ideas around AI safety, and offer concrete strategies to make your generative AI features safe, reliable, and delightful.
