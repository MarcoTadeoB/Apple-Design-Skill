> Descripción de Apple: Learn how to create shared experiences for people wearing Vision Pro in the same room. We'll show you how to integrate SharePlay and leverage ARKit in your app, introduce the updated window sharing flows for nearby and FaceTime participants, and cover new API designed for seamless collaboration. Discover best practices to make your collaborative features stand out, easily discoverable, and engaging for people together in the same space.
