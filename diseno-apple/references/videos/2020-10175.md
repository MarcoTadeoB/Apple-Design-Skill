> Descripción de Apple: Learn how to achieve exceptional typography in your app's user interface that enhances legibility, accessibility, and consistency across Apple platforms. Get up to speed on the latest advancements to the San Francisco font family including the move to variable fonts for accommodating optical sizes and weights. We'll also share tips about how to get the most out of systems fonts, support dynamic type with custom fonts. For a refresher on the principles behind the San Francisco font family, catch up on “Introducing the New System Fonts” from WWDC15.
