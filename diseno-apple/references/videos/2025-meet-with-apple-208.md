> Descripción de Apple: Learn directly from developers about how they implemented the new design and Liquid Glass across Apple platforms. In this series of online presentations by third-party teams, you'll find out how developers of all sizes are embracing native design, expressing their brand identities, and quickly and successfully adopting the latest features. This activity also includes a panel discussion with Apple designers. Conducted in English.
