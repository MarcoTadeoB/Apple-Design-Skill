> Descripción de Apple: Learn how to build privacy into your apps from the planning stages through deployment. We'll cover practical ways to integrate privacy at each step of the development lifecycle, focusing on data minimization, on-device processing, and transparency and control. You'll discover how to use Apple's tools and frameworks to protect user data and create a privacy-respecting app experience.
