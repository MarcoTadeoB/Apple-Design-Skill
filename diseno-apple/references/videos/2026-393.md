Learn to craft dynamic materials with Shader Graph, blend skeletal animations with Animation Graph, and simulate particle effects with Compute Graph. Go further by wiring up app interactivity with Script Graph, coordinating scene events with Sequencer, and designing intelligent NPC behaviors with Behavior Trees — all without leaving the editor.
