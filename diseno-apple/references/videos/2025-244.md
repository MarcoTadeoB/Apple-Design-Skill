> Descripción de Apple: Learn about the App Intents framework and its increasingly critical role within Apple's developer platforms. We'll take you through a ground-up introduction of the core concepts: intents, entities, queries, and much more. You'll learn how these pieces fit together and let you integrate your app through Apple's devices, from software features like Spotlight and Shortcuts to hardware features like the Action button. We'll also walk through how App Intents is your app's gateway to integrating with Apple Intelligence going forward.
