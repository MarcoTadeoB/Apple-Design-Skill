> Descripción de Apple: iPhone and iPad support numerous input modes for those with motor impairments, including touch interaction modification, Switch Control, and Full Keyboard Access. We'll explore how people can interact with their devices solely through keyboard input, working through a real-life example to discover key APIs. We'll also take you through some best practices for supporting motor accessibility when you integrate Full Keyboard Access in your apps.
