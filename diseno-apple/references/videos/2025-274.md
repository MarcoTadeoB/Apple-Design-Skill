> Descripción de Apple: Discover how to seamlessly blend SwiftUI and RealityKit in visionOS 26. We'll explore enhancements to Model3D, including animation and ConfigurationCatalog support, and demonstrate smooth transitions to RealityView. You'll learn how to leverage SwiftUI animations to drive RealityKit component changes, implement interactive manipulation, use new SwiftUI components for richer interactions, and observe RealityKit changes from your SwiftUI code. We'll also cover how to use unified coordinate conversion for cross-framework coordinate transformations.
