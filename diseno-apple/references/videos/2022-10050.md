> Descripción de Apple: Our widgets code-along returns as we adventure onto the watchOS and iOS Lock Screen. Learn about the latest improvements to WidgetKit that help power complex complications on watchOS and can help you create Lock Screen widgets for iPhone. We'll show you how to incorporate the latest SwiftUI views to provide great glanceable data, explore how each platform renders content, and learn how you can customize the design and feel of your content within a widget or complication.
